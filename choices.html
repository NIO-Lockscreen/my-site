Your Privacy Choices

Beat the Odds is built around a simple principle — your data is yours, and we don't want it.

I.

Leaderboard Participation

Submitting a score is always optional. You can play the entire game, beat every streak, and never send anything to our servers. If you skip the leaderboard, no data ever leaves your device.

II.

Your Display Name

If you do submit a score, you choose what name appears. Use a nickname, an emoji, gibberish — whatever you like. We recommend not using your real name.

III.

Removing a Submission

Changed your mind about a leaderboard entry? Email daiwizsoftware@gmail.com with the display name and approximate submission time, and we'll remove it.

IV.

Local Data

All your in-game progress, streaks, prestige upgrades, and settings live on your device. Deleting the app removes them. We have no copies.

V.

Tracking & Ads

There are none. No analytics, no ad networks, no third-party SDKs watching what you do. Anti-subscription. Anti-surveillance. Always.
